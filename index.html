The games

Play in your browser. No account or installation.

A1 · Digital devices

Device Division ↗

Dispatch devices to clients, handle breakdowns and submit equipment bids within a budget.

5 rounds · about 90 minutes

A1.3–A5 · Devices and systems

Systems Workshop ↗

Fit components and run the bench tests. Check USB ports, speed bottlenecks, controller delay and a flowchart’s two paths.

6 builds · equipment and compatibility

B · Transmitting data

Packet Route ↗

Route two streams on the map. Recover from a broken link, compress a live feed and resolve shared bottlenecks.

5 networks · traffic and failover

C · Operating online

Community Shift ↗

Deploy services, assign access to four roles and staff a moderation queue. Rehearse a shift to see where your setup fails.

3 shifts · cloud services and permissions

D · Protecting data

Defence Desk ↗

Inspect affected systems, act on the right asset and verify a clean restore. Every operation takes time.

5 incidents · containment and recovery

E · Impact of IT systems

Service Launch ↗

Stack rules in a form builder, try your own records and run boundary tests before approving the release.

4 forms · validation and verification

F · Ethics and law

Policy Files ↗

Redact a public export, allocate licences and route retired equipment. Review the effect on the organisation and its users.

7 files · workplace decisions
